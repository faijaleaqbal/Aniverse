Frequently Asked Questions

Everything most people ask about AniFlick, answered honestly. If your question is not here, check the Contact page.

What is AniFlick?

AniFlick is a free anime streaming site. Its catalog is powered by AniList and covers around 12,944 titles — series, movies, OVAs, ONAs and specials — with Sub and Dub audio where available. There is no subscription and no paywall.

Is AniFlick really free?

Yes. Watching is completely free, and there are no premium tiers, no locked episodes, and no trial that runs out. The site itself shows no ads.

Do I need an account to watch?

No. You can browse and watch everything without signing in. An account only adds personal features: a watchlist, watch history, continue-watching, episode reminders, and comments. You can sign up with an email and password, or with Google, Discord, MyAnimeList, or AniList.

Why do some titles not play?

AniFlick does not host any video files — every episode streams from a third-party player. Around 70% of catalog titles have a working stream; the rest are titles no player currently carries, mostly older or very obscure ones. If one server fails, try another from the Server row on the watch page. Episodes that aired very recently can also take a while to appear on the players.

What is the difference between Sub and Dub?

Sub is the original Japanese audio with English subtitles. Dub is English voice acting. Not every title has a dub — where a dub exists you can switch audio from the watch page. Some titles are Sub-only on every player.

How do I report a broken episode?

There is no report button yet. First, try the other servers on the watch page — a title that fails on one player often works on another. If no server plays it, the episode is not on the players right now; checking back later sometimes helps, as players add missing episodes over time.

Why do I see ads or popups in the player?

Ads inside the video player come from the third-party player itself, and the site cannot reach inside another site's player to remove them. Popup ads, however, are blocked: the players run in a sandboxed frame that stops popups from opening. The AniFlick pages themselves carry no advertising.

Can I use AniFlick on my phone?

Yes. The site works in any modern mobile browser — no app to install. The watch page, controls, and fullscreen all work on phones; if fullscreen ever misbehaves on your device, rotating to landscape usually fixes it.

What do I get by creating an account?

A personal watchlist, watch history, continue-watching across devices, reminders for upcoming episodes, and the ability to comment. Your account data lives in AniFlick's own database — social logins only verify your identity and never receive your AniFlick data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are stored only as hashes, never in plain text. Logins use a random session token stored in a cookie, and the session expires after 30 days of inactivity. We do not sell personal data and we do not run advertising trackers. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
