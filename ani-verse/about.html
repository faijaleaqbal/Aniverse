About AniFlick

AniFlick is a free anime streaming site built for people who just want to watch anime — no subscription, no paywall, no account required.

What it is

AniFlick lets you browse and watch anime in your browser. Open a title, pick an episode, and it plays. The interface is inspired by the clean layouts of modern streaming sites, with a dark theme, a light theme, and a mobile-friendly watch page.

The catalog

The catalog is powered by AniList and covers around 12,944 titles: TV series, movies, OVAs, ONAs, and specials — currently airing shows, finished classics, and everything in between. You can browse by genre, search by name, follow the broadcast schedule, or jump to a random title. Adult (hentai) titles are never listed.

Players

Episodes stream from third-party players embedded on the watch page, with Sub and Dub audio where available. If one player fails, you can switch to another from the Server row — the site never switches players on its own. Around 70% of catalog titles currently have a working stream; the rest are titles no player carries yet.

Accounts

Watching needs no account. Signing up — with email and password, or with Google, Discord, MyAnimeList, or AniList — adds a watchlist, watch history, continue-watching, episode reminders, and comments. See the FAQ and Privacy Policy for more.

The project

AniFlick is an independent project built by anime fans, for anime fans. It is not affiliated with AniList, MyAnimeList, or any studio or licensor. Anime titles, artwork, and video content belong to their respective owners.
