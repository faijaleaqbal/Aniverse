Privacy Policy

AniFlick is a free anime streaming site. This policy explains what data we collect, what we do not collect, and who else is involved when you use the site.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What we collect

Account data (only if you create an account). Your email address, display name, and a password hash — never your password in plain text. If you sign in with Google, Discord, MyAnimeList, or AniList, we store the provider name and your provider account ID so we can recognise you next time; the provider only confirms your identity and returns your name and email.

Your activity (only while logged in). Your watchlist, watch history, episode progress, reminders, and comments. Signed-out visitors generate none of this.

Session records. When you log in, the server creates a session record holding a random token, your user ID, an expiry date, and the IP address and browser (user agent) seen at login. This is what keeps you logged in and lets us spot session abuse.

What we do not collect

We do not run analytics trackers or advertising trackers on the site. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share your account data with advertisers. We do not collect payment information — the service is free and there is nothing to buy.

Cookies and local storage

  • av_session (cookie): the random session token described above. It is set only when you log in, lasts 30 days, and is extended by another 30 days when you visit while fewer than 7 days remain. Logging out deletes the session immediately.
  • av_theme (local storage): your light/dark theme choice, stored only in your own browser.
  • av_reminders (local storage): episode reminders you set, stored only in your own browser.

Clearing cookies or local storage removes these from your device; clearing the session cookie signs you out.

Third-party services

AniList. The catalog, search results, schedules, and title details come from the AniList API, queried by our server. Your browser does not send your personal data to AniList.

Video players. Episodes play inside third-party player embeds (such as Zoko and TVplus). Those players are separate services with their own privacy policies; AniFlick cannot see what they collect. Their in-player ads are theirs, not ours.

Social login providers. Google, Discord, MyAnimeList, and AniList verify your identity at sign-in. They never receive your AniFlick data — your watchlist, history, and comments stay in our database.

Data security

Passwords are hashed with a one-way function, sessions use unguessable random tokens, and the site is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep the data we hold to the minimum needed to run the service.

Your choices and data requests

You can log out at any time, which ends your session immediately. To ask about, correct, or delete your account data, reach out on our Discord server — join the server and DM a moderator, and tell us the email address on the account so we can find it. We will confirm the request belongs to you before acting on it. We do not offer email support.
